Cat Allergies: Practical Ways to Reduce Symptoms

Living with a cat allergy can be frustrating, especially if you love spending time around pets. Sneezing, itchy eyes and a stuffy nose may appear after a visit to a home with cats—or in your own home. Knowing what actually triggers the symptoms can make it easier to choose what helps.

What causes a cat allergy?

Most reactions are not caused by fur itself. Cats produce proteins, including Fel d 1, that are found in their saliva and skin secretions. These proteins can collect on fur, clothing, bedding and furniture. Tiny particles may remain in indoor air or settle on surfaces even after the cat has left the room.

Changes at home that may help

Consider keeping the bedroom free of cats so you have a lower-exposure place to sleep. Wash bedding regularly, clean surfaces with a damp cloth and use a vacuum fitted with a HEPA filter if available. A HEPA air cleaner may help reduce airborne particles, although it cannot remove all allergens. Have someone without cat allergies handle brushing where possible, and wash your hands after touching pets.

These measures may reduce exposure, but they do not make a cat-allergic home completely allergen-free. No cat breed has been proven to be truly hypoallergenic.

Treatment options to discuss

Depending on your symptoms, a clinician or pharmacist may recommend a non-drowsy antihistamine, a steroid nasal spray or suitable eye drops. Nasal sprays often work best when used consistently and correctly. Some people with persistent symptoms may benefit from an assessment for allergy immunotherapy. Treatment decisions depend on age, other health conditions and the severity of the reaction.

When to get medical advice

If your symptoms keep interrupting sleep, school or work—or if you wheeze or have asthma—arrange a clinical assessment. Breathing difficulty or other signs of a severe reaction require urgent medical attention.

Cat allergies vary from person to person. A practical plan combines realistic exposure reduction with treatments matched to your symptoms, rather than relying on a single home remedy.
